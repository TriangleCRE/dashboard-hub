Every dashboard on the Hub, in one table: when it was last updated, when it's next due, who's the one person that can edit it in Claude Code, and — click Sources or Instructions — where its data comes from and how to actually make an update. Click any Owner, Last updated, Next update due, Sources, or Instructions value to edit it right here — no form to open. Under "Next update due," open the checklist to see (or add) the specific reports/tasks due and check one off as you finish it — that automatically updates "Last updated" too. Adding a dashboard here (or on the Hub) adds it to both; any edit made here updates the Hub too, since both pages read and write the exact same record.
